About Kitakits DVO

Everything you need to enjoy Davao’s events, in one place.

Kitakits DVO is an event discovery and planning application designed to help people explore activities happening around Davao City, organize their schedules, and prepare for their outings in one place.

  • Easier discovery
  • More organized planning
  • More enjoyable outings

Features

What you can do with Kitakits DVO

Discover Local Events

Explore concerts, festivals, campus activities, sports, food events, arts, and community gatherings.

Find Events That Match You

Browse and filter events according to available categories and preferences.

Save or Register for Events

Keep track of events you are interested in and access relevant event registration details.

Plan Your Davao Day

Organize selected events into an itinerary and manage your schedule.

Estimate Your Budget

Prepare for expected expenses, such as transportation and food.

Track Actual Expenses

Record spending to help you monitor your event-day budget.
